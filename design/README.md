Design files for 3D printed star and flower diffusers, as well as laser cut files for faux leather cover.
Print diffusers out of translucent PETG (ideally) filament. I like to use [California Filament Cloudy White](https://californiafilament.com/products/cloudy-white-petg-filament-1). Stop the printafter *2.4 mm* and add a layer of tulle or mesh (mosquito netting works well) before completing the print. The flat faces of the diffusers should rest against the print bed. For best outcome with the sloped sides around the indentation where the faux leather cover is seated, use an adaptive layer height setting if your slicer supports it, or use a small enough layer height that overhangs aren't a problem.
Laser cut the covers from laserable leatherette or any other flexible, laser cuttable material.

## Design files for 3D printed star and flower diffusers + outlines for faux leather covers <BR><BR>

### 3D Printed Diffuser Array
- Print diffusers out of translucent PETG (ideally) filament. I like to use [California Filament Cloudy White](https://californiafilament.com/products/cloudy-white-petg-filament-1).
- Stop the printafter *2.4 mm* and add a layer of tulle or mesh (mosquito netting works well) before completing the print. The flat faces of the diffusers should rest against the print bed.
- For best outcome with the sloped sides around the indentation where the faux leather cover is seated, use an adaptive layer height setting if your slicer supports it, or use a small enough layer height that overhangs aren't a problem.

### Laser Cut Faux Leather Cover
- Laser cut the covers from laserable leatherette or any other flexible, laser cuttable material
- Houston acrylic has a [nice selection of intersting faux leather fabric](https://houstonacrylic.com/collections/leatherette)

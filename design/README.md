Design files for 3D printed star and flower diffusers, as well as laser cut files for faux leather cover.
Print diffusers out of translucent PETG (ideally) filament. I like to use [California Filament Cloudy White](https://californiafilament.com/products/cloudy-white-petg-filament-1). Stop the printafter 1.2 mm and add a 
layer of tulle or mesh (mosquito netting works well) before completing the print.
Laser cut the covers from laserable leatherette or any other flexible, laser cuttable material.
